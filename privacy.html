Privacy Policy

ClutchClip Publisher · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information ClutchClip Publisher (“the App”, “we”, “us”) accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how you can revoke access. The App is operated by Clutch Clip (contact: clutchclip.official@gmail.com) and is used only to manage the Clutch Clip channel's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API). By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information we access

When an account owner signs in with Google or TikTok (OAuth) and grants permission, the App can access:

  • YouTube: the basic channel profile (channel ID and name) of the signed-in account; the ability to upload videos to that channel; and statistics for that channel's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time, average view percentage).
  • TikTok: the basic profile (open ID, display name, username) of the signed-in account; the ability to upload videos or drafts to that account; and statistics for that account's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares).

The App does not access any other user's account, private messages, subscribers' personal information, or payment information.

3. How we use it

  • to publish short clips to the connected Clutch Clip channel/account;
  • to show the account owner how those clips perform and to improve which moments are selected in the future.

We do not sell, rent or use this information for advertising, and we do not use it to build profiles of viewers.

4. Storage and security

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted (Fernet/AES) in the App's private database and are never shared.
  • Video statistics and the App's own publishing records (titles, captions, post links) are stored in the same private database.
  • Access to the App's dashboard is password-protected and limited to the Clutch Clip team.

5. Sharing with service providers

To create subtitles and captions, the audio and transcript text of videos that Clutch Clip owns or is licensed to use may be processed by an AI service provider (such as Groq or OpenAI) under that provider's terms. No YouTube or TikTok account data (tokens, statistics, profile data) is sent to these providers. We do not otherwise share information with third parties, except where required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data obtained from YouTube API Services is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. Statistics are refreshed periodically, and stored statistics older than 30 days are deleted or refreshed, in line with the YouTube API Services policies.
  • When access is revoked, the App deletes the stored tokens and stops collecting data. Remaining stored data for that account is deleted within 30 days, or sooner on request.
  • To request deletion, email clutchclip.official@gmail.com.

7. Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: remove the App's access at any time from the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/connections (also reachable at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app go to Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions, and remove ClutchClip Publisher.

8. Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The App's private dashboard uses a single login cookie for the Clutch Clip team.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not used to collect information from children.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: clutchclip.official@gmail.com.
