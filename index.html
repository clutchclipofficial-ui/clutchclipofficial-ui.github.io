Short clips of the best gaming moments.

Clutch Clip is a short-form video channel on YouTube Shorts and TikTok. We turn original gameplay and officially licensed footage (for example, footage provided through creator clipping programs) into vertical clips with subtitles.

ClutchClip Publisher

ClutchClip Publisher is the private tool the Clutch Clip team uses to publish its own clips to its own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It is not offered to the public. It:

  • edits videos we own or are licensed to use into vertical clips (cropping, subtitles, captions);
  • uploads those clips to our own YouTube channel and TikTok account through the official YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API, after we sign in with OAuth;
  • reads view, like, comment and share counts for our own uploads through the YouTube Analytics API and TikTok Display API, so we can see which clips perform best.

It never accesses anyone else's account, never downloads videos from YouTube, and never posts content we don't have the rights to.
